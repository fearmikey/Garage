# Contributing to Garage

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Garage!

## 🛡️ FOSS & F-Droid Compliance Policy

Garage is strictly **Free and Open Source Software (FOSS)** and is maintained for distribution on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org) and other open-source repositories.

To maintain compliance with F-Droid's Inclusion Policy and anti-feature guidelines:

1. **No Proprietary Dependencies:** Pull requests or prompts introducing Google Play Services, AdMob/Google Mobile Ads, ML Kit, Firebase, or any non-free binary SDKs will be rejected.
2. **No Tracking or Ads:** Garage contains zero ads, zero analytics, and zero telemetry.
3. **Offline-First Privacy:** Features must work without mandatory cloud dependencies or non-free backend services.
4. **Open Source Libraries Only:** Any new third-party dependency must be published under a recognized FOSS license (Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD, MPL, GPL) and contain no non-free binary components.
